      The Rebrand Risk Index

      What might be renamed next?

      The index weighs current-name age (45%), prior identities (35%), and family rename frequency (20%). It measures historical rebrand pressure, not probability. Corporate naming committees remain stubbornly non-deterministic.

      How this was calculated

      Enough method to keep the joke honest

      Measured

      All figures are calculated in the browser from the canonical registry. Durations use completed name periods; current-name age ends at the dataset’s “as of” date. Medians limit distortion from unusually durable names.

      Limited

      The registry includes surviving products only, so discontinued products are absent and the analysis has survivorship bias. Family sizes differ, partial dates retain their documented precision, and correlation is not a secret product roadmap.

      Predicted, technically

      Scores are deterministic rankings of three historical signals. Status bands are deliberately broad: ● Elevated, ◐ Watchlist and ○ Not imminent. A high score means “good setup for a joke,” not “confirmed for Build.”
