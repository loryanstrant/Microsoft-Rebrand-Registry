What this registry includes

Every entry is a product that still exists today; former names are shown only as the history of that continuing product. Discontinued products are not listed. Product or platform transformations—where something evolved into, was absorbed by, or was replaced by something else—are also outside scope. This registry tracks documented renames, not broader lineage.

About the dates

Periods use the most precise date supported by available evidence. “By” means a source confirms the name was in use by that month, but may not establish its exact first day. Ongoing durations are calculated to the dataset’s “as of” date.

Some rename leads were drawn from M365 Maps and Rebranded by Microsoft, then checked against the cited sources.
